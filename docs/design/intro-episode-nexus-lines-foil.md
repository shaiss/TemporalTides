## Line rules

- Only the active character speaks. One line per look, per action, or per broken plan.
- A line says what that character perceives and what limits them. It never names who should act.
- Lines are short enough to read at a glance: at most 26 words.
- No designer vocabulary in any line: puzzle, step, station, beat numbers.
- Each line cites the canon it rests on.

Moments: **look** = the active character looks at a station; **can act** = their lens lights something to tap; **after** = line after acting; **break** = the gate plan stops at this character.

## Where the rules ran out

Writing these lines tested how far the canon keeps an author on model. These are the places it was silent, ambiguous or in tension, with the call made for this draft.

| # | Gap | Call made here | Suggested canon fix |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Anne and Maya sound alike when stating timing.** Both are "precise" in canon; Anne's distinct markers (likelihoods, contingencies) live only in her verbal tics. | Anne speaks in likelihoods and "what if"; Maya in observed sequences and measurements. 4 of Anne's 6 lines lean on a tic to stay distinct. | Add to the narrative-structure dialogue rules: Anne states *likely futures and contingencies*; Maya states *observed sequences and measures*. |
| 2 | **Anne and Eli share a verbal tic.** Both dossiers list "Okay, so". | Only Anne uses it here. | Give each character tics of their own. |
| 3 | **Vibrion has no dialogue examples** in his dossier and no entry in the narrative-structure dialogue techniques, though the Writing Guidelines point there for "Vibrion's electrical language". | Voice built from his speech patterns and tics ("out of phase", "frequency", "couple"). 5 of his 12 lines use a tic. | Add Vibrion communication examples to his dossier and a dialogue-techniques entry. |
| 4 | **Abilities overlap the single-lens stations.** Maya has Trap Detection; Anne has pattern recognition; Vibrion has limited precognition, pattern recognition, object manipulation and electrical disruption. | Each "can't" line names a concrete condition: corridor discharge is temporal, not current; intervals do not repeat; the levers' problem is order, not force; the dead panel has nothing running to observe. | Station specs state the limiting condition that keeps other lenses out. |
| 5 | **Vibrion's own reserves** (about 200 MJ stored) are not bounded against external devices. | The seal "needs more than I'm carrying"; its grid feed runs through the dead panel. | A rule of thumb for when stored energy is or is not enough. |
| 6 | **Cross-lens persistence** is undefined: can Eli see the marks Maya made? | Marks persist in every lens once made. | State what one character's action leaves visible to the others. |
| 7 | **Staging of inactive characters** is undefined: are the other three in the room? | Lines assume the team is present ("Did anyone see that?", "Good work, all of you"), matching storyboard Panel 6 "Team together". | Decide whether inactive characters appear in the chamber. |
| 8 | **Head-hopping** is defined for prose only. | One speaker at a time; a lens switch is the section break. | Add the game equivalent to the POV rules. |
| 9 | **Maya boundaries:** "puzzle" metaphors are to be avoided, yet her dossier lists "Puzzle Solving" as a strength; savant stereotypes are to be avoided, yet her canon examples recite exact measurements. | No "puzzle" in any line; her numbers come from watching, with stated limits ("I would need much longer to observe one"). | Clarify that "puzzle" is a system term, never a metaphor for Maya; define the line between precision and savant framing. |
| 10 | **No game-dialogue guidance** on line length or how often verbal tics may appear. | At most 26 words. Tic frequency left to judgement (see gaps 1 and 3). | Add a short-form voice note per character for barks and subtitles. |
| 11 | **Hand-off verbs** (Anne marks, Maya locks, Eli hits, Vibrion couples) come from the beat sheet, not the dossiers. | Used as the beat sheet words them. | Describe each hand-off in the ability sections. |
