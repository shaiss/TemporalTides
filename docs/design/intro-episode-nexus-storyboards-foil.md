## Panels

### Panel 0 — Arrive

**Beat:** 0 · Narrative · Vibrion frame  
**Frame:** Wide: Time Nexus chamber, clocks slightly out of sync. Vibrion silhouette holds the frame. Entropy = edge shadow only.  
**Caption:** “One lens at a time.”

### Panel 1 — Anne / hazard

**Beat:** 1 · Perception / hazard · Anne (Foresight)  
**Frame:** Corridor; Anne’s foresight overlay (ghosted trap silhouettes / timing ticks). Other modes = noise.  
**Caption:** Hazard options only she sees.

### Panel 2 — Maya / marks

**Beat:** 2 · Sequence · Maya (Pattern Sense)  
**Frame:** Echo levers; Maya’s marks on relative strain / wear. Sequence readable only in her lens.  
**Caption:** She *marks* the pattern — others can’t see that detail.

### Panel 3 — Eli / window

**Beat:** 3 · Speed · Eli (Kinetic Rush)  
**Frame:** Same levers; clock window collapsing. Eli’s rush = motion blur / timeline bend completing Maya’s sequence.  
**Caption:** Faster than careful hands.

### Panel 4 — Vibrion / panel

**Beat:** 4 · Energy · Vibrion (Vibrational Manipulation)  
**Frame:** Dead electrical panel; world-grid hum. Stuck lever; Eli can help shoulder, Vibrion must couple.  
**Caption:** Connected to the world’s components.

### Panel 5 — Coordination gate

**Beat:** 5 · Coordination · All four  
**Frame:** Four clock faces disagree. Ordered steps: Anne tick → Maya lock → Eli window → Vibrion seal. Solo force = reject.  
**Caption:** Friction from different timelines — sync clocks, don’t invent drama.

### Panel 6 — Resonance tease

**Beat:** 6 · Team / exit  
**Frame:** Team together; Maya’s pattern visible under Vibrion’s leadership. Entropy still open at the edge.  
**Caption:** Resonance, not victory.
