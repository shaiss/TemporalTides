# Temporal Puzzle Design

**Part IX: Gameplay Mechanics**

---

## Overview

Puzzles in Temporal Tides are rooted in temporal mechanics and character abilities. Every puzzle has multiple valid solutions (different characters approach differently). Puzzles should teach empathy: experiencing a challenge through different perceptions reveals different truths.

---

## Puzzle Types

### 1. Perception Puzzles
**Mechanic:** Requires seeing time/reality differently
**Example:** A blocked path is only blocked in one reality layer. Switch to Iris → see alternate route.
**Characters:** Iris (primary), Leo (analytical), Dave (emotional reading)
**Thematic:** Different perspectives reveal different truths

### 2. Coordination Puzzles
**Mechanic:** Multiple characters act in different timestreams
**Example:** Anne predicts timing; Eli executes at speed; Maya confirms sequence
**Characters:** Team-based (minimum 2; best with 3+)
**Thematic:** Synergy through diversity

### 3. Sequence Puzzles
**Mechanic:** Pattern recognition across temporal phenomena
**Example:** Temporal echoes replay events in wrong order. Maya identifies correct sequence.
**Characters:** Maya (primary), Leo (analytical), Anne (predictive)
**Thematic:** Order within chaos; pattern as strength

### 4. Paradox Puzzles
**Mechanic:** Resolving temporal contradictions
**Example:** Two timeline versions of same room; both "true." Reconcile through understanding both.
**Characters:** Iris (see both), Leo (analyze contradiction), Dave (feel emotional truth)
**Thematic:** Multiple truths can coexist

### 5. Memory Puzzles
**Mechanic:** Accessing temporal echoes for information
**Example:** Past event replaying in location; extract information from echo
**Characters:** Iris (see echoes), Dave (feel emotional echoes), Leo (analyze content)
**Thematic:** Past informs present

### 6. Energy Puzzles
**Mechanic:** Manipulate energy to affect temporal mechanics
**Example:** Power a device to stabilize a gradient; route energy through circuit
**Characters:** Vibrion (primary; energy manipulation)
**Thematic:** Power and responsibility

### 7. Empathy Puzzles
**Mechanic:** Understand NPC emotional/temporal state to progress
**Example:** NPC hiding information; Dave reads emotional truth; correct response unlocks path
**Characters:** Dave (primary), Anne (anticipate), Iris (perceive layers)
**Thematic:** Understanding through connection

---

## Design Principles

### Multiple Solutions
- Every puzzle solvable by at least 2 characters (different approaches)
- "Best" solution varies (speed vs. depth vs. elegance)
- No single correct answer

### Difficulty Progression
- Act I: Single-ability; tutorial
- Act II: Multi-step; character switching
- Act III: Team coordination; complex
- Act IV: Reflection puzzles (emotional, not mechanical)

### Accessibility
- Hints available after time threshold (adjustable)
- No time pressure by default (option to add)
- Multiple difficulty levels affect hint frequency, not puzzle content
- Alternate solutions for accessibility

### Temporal Integration
- Puzzles use zone properties (RFR affects timing)
- Character perception changes puzzle presentation
- Same puzzle looks different through different eyes
