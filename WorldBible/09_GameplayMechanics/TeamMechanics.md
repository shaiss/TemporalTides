# Team Mechanics

**Part IX: Gameplay Mechanics**

---

## Overview

Team mechanics translate the narrative theme — neurodiversity as strength — into gameplay. Characters' abilities combine, complement, and amplify each other. No single character is sufficient; the team is greater than its parts.

---

## Synergy System

### How Synergies Work
- When two characters act in coordinated sequence, synergy bonus activates
- Synergies discovered through play (not listed in menu until first use)
- Each pairing has unique synergy reflecting their relationship

### Key Synergies

**Vibrion + Eli (Energy Rush):**
- Vibrion charges; Eli channels speed through energy field
- Effect: Devastating combined attack; area-clearing
- Relationship: Natural camaraderie amplifies

**Anne + Maya (Strategic Pattern):**
- Anne predicts; Maya confirms sequence
- Effect: Perfect navigation through complex hazards
- Relationship: Complementary thinking (anticipation + structure)

**Leo + Iris (Deep Vision):**
- Leo's analysis + Iris's perception
- Effect: Reveal hidden content at deeper level than either alone
- Relationship: Intellectual curiosity meets multi-reality

**Dave + Eli (Grounded Speed):**
- Dave's empathy buffs; Eli's speed
- Effect: Eli with emotional awareness (doesn't crash; reads room)
- Relationship: Opposite energies balancing

**Dave + Iris (Empathic Vision):**
- Dave senses emotion; Iris sees layers
- Effect: Emotional truth across reality layers (deepest reading possible)
- Relationship: Both see what others miss

**Anne + Vibrion (Tactical Command):**
- Anne plans; Vibrion executes with precision
- Effect: Optimized team deployment; best outcomes
- Relationship: Deputy + leader

**Maya + Leo (Pattern Analysis):**
- Maya finds pattern; Leo analyzes meaning
- Effect: Solve previously impossible puzzles
- Relationship: Different but complementary analytical styles

## Team Coordination Challenges

### Multi-Character Puzzles
- Player assigns characters to positions/roles
- Each character performs their part in sequence
- Timing matters (temporal coordination)
- Failure: retry with different assignment or timing

### Communication System
- Characters call out observations (automated; character voice)
- Player receives info filtered through current character's perception
- Switching characters mid-challenge provides different info

---

## Character Switching Strategy

### When to Switch
- Obstacle requires different ability
- Current character overwhelmed (environmental stress)
- Synergy opportunity available
- Better perception needed for current situation

### Switching Cost
- Brief transition moment (1-2 seconds; not instantaneous)
- Cannot switch during active ability use
- Some zones restrict switching (narrative)

## Relationship Mechanics

### Relationship Level
- Deepens through: missions together, dialogue choices, synergy use
- Higher relationship = stronger synergy bonuses
- Maximum relationship unlocks unique combined ability

### Team Morale
- Affected by: mission outcomes, Dr. Mental sessions, rest, story events
- High morale: ability bonuses; better coordination
- Low morale: reduced effectiveness; character-specific debuffs
