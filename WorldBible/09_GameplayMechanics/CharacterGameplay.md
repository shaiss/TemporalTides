# Character-Specific Gameplay

**Part IX: Gameplay Mechanics**

---

## Overview

Each character plays differently. Switching characters is not cosmetic — it fundamentally changes how the player perceives and interacts with the world. Abilities are drawn from character dossier "Gameplay Applications" sections.

---

## Vibrion — Leader / Energy
**Play Style:** Action-oriented; energy management
**Primary Mechanic:** Vibrational Manipulation
- **Low Frequency:** Sensing (reveals hidden elements), communication
- **Medium Frequency:** Enhanced combat, object manipulation
- **High Frequency:** Shockwaves, phasing (brief), bioelectric disruption
- **Extreme Frequency:** Reality-bending (rare; costly; dangerous)
**Resource:** Energy meter (absorb to recharge; deplete through use)
**Unique:** Can power devices, open energy-locked doors, detect temporal anomalies
**Weakness:** Enigma Volt encounter = disrupted controls, degraded ability
**Role:** Combat primary; energy puzzles; leadership decisions

---

## Leo — Analyst / Deep Focus
**Play Style:** Puzzle-focused; contemplative
**Primary Mechanic:** Immersive Thought
- **Activation:** Hold focus button; world slows/freezes; internal analysis begins
- **Effect:** Complex puzzles reveal connections; abstract concepts link
- **Duration:** Limited by focus meter (depletes during use; recovers over time)
**Unique:** Can solve multi-variable puzzles others cannot; "deep dive" reveals hidden lore
**Weakness:** Disrupted by noise, interruption; vulnerable while focused
**Role:** Analytical puzzles; research; lore discovery

---

## Anne — Strategist / Foresight
**Play Style:** Planning; risk assessment
**Primary Mechanic:** Foresight
- **Activation:** Scan environment; possible futures overlay
- **Effect:** Hazards highlighted; optimal paths shown; enemy patterns predicted
- **Duration:** Stress meter (overuse causes overload; screen narrows)
**Unique:** Can anticipate traps, enemy actions, environmental hazards
**Weakness:** Information overload in chaotic zones; paralysis if too many variables
**Role:** Strategic planning; hazard navigation; team coordination

---

## Maya — Navigator / Pattern
**Play Style:** Sequence-based; precise
**Primary Mechanic:** Pattern Sense
- **Activation:** Automatic; patterns highlight in environment
- **Effect:** Temporal rhythms visible; sequence puzzles solvable; safe paths through gradients
- **Duration:** Passive (always partially active); deep focus for complex patterns
**Unique:** Detects temporal loops, gradient cliffs, hidden sequences
**Weakness:** Sensory overload in chaotic environments; needs structured approach
**Role:** Navigation; sequence puzzles; temporal rhythm challenges

---

## Eli — Speedster / Action
**Play Style:** High-speed; reflex-based
**Primary Mechanic:** Kinetic Rush
- **Activation:** Burst trigger; world slows while Eli accelerates
- **Effect:** Rapid movement; enhanced reflexes; time-sensitive challenges
- **Duration:** Brief (30-60 seconds); cooldown required
**Unique:** Can reach time-limited objectives; rapid escort; combat dodging
**Weakness:** Unpredictable in variable-RFR zones; energy crash after use
**Role:** Speed challenges; combat support; rapid response

---

## Dave — Support / Empathy
**Play Style:** Social; emotional; support
**Primary Mechanic:** Empathic Resonance
- **Activation:** Passive sensing; active probe (hold button)
- **Effect:** NPC emotional states visible; deception detected; team emotional buffs
- **Duration:** Passive always on; active drains emotional meter
**Unique:** Read NPC truth/lies; detect hidden emotional content; team healing
**Weakness:** Overwhelmed in crowds; depressive episodes reduce effectiveness
**Role:** Social puzzles; NPC interaction; team support; emotional challenges

---

## Iris — Perception / Multi-Reality
**Play Style:** Exploration; hidden content
**Primary Mechanic:** Visionary Insight
- **Activation:** Toggle layer visibility; multiple realities overlay
- **Effect:** Hidden elements revealed; temporal echoes visible; alternate paths seen
- **Duration:** Sustained (but disorienting over time)
**Unique:** See through walls (different reality layer); find hidden passages; temporal echoes
**Weakness:** Distinguishing real from vision (unreliable narrator mechanic); overwhelm
**Role:** Hidden content; reality puzzles; temporal echo challenges; Lair navigation
