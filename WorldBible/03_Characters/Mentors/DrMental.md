# Dr. Mental - Psycho-Temporal Therapist & Advisor

**Part III: Characters | Mentors**

---

### Quick Reference

**Full Name:** Dr. Priya Mental
**Age:** 55 years (born 287 AC)
**Temporal Signature:** Class A (Standard) — baseline perception; professional neutrality
**Role:** Chief Psycho-Temporal Therapist, Time Nexus; Team advisor
**Visual Reference:** ![Dr. Mental](/assets/character_portraits/Dr._Mental.jpg)

**Biography:**

**Origin:**

Priya Mental grew up in a dilated zone — a contemplative community where time moved slowly and introspection was cultural norm. Her childhood was marked by observation: she noticed how her neighbors experienced time differently, how some struggled with the slowness, how others thrived. That curiosity shaped her path.

**Academic and Clinical Training:**

- **Age 18-28:** Psychology and neuroscience (Baseline Metropolitan University)
- **Age 25:** Studied under the researcher who discovered bioelectric field interactions with temporal flow (per Universal Timeline) — foundational work for psycho-temporal field
- **Age 28-32:** Clinical training in neurodivergent-affirming therapy
- **Age 32:** Published first paper linking temporal perception to mental health conditions; controversy and breakthrough

**Establishing the Practice:**

- **Age 35 (322 AC):** Established practice at Time Nexus specializing in neuro-temporal psychology
- **Age 38:** Named Chief Psycho-Temporal Therapist (Nexus recognition of field importance)
- **Age 40-present:** Documented neuro-temporal abilities systematically; developed assessment and support protocols
- Pioneered the understanding that mental health conditions and temporal abilities are interconnected — not cause-and-effect, but mutually informing

**Relationship to the Team:**

- **Vibrion:** Initial contact when Vibrion assembled team; conducted group assessment, ongoing consultation on team dynamics
- **Individual members:** Each has worked with her for ability-condition integration (Anne, Maya, Eli, Dave, Iris, Leo — all have sessions)
- **Role:** Therapist, advisor, advocate. She does not direct missions. She supports the people running them.

**Current Status:**

Chief Psycho-Temporal Therapist at Time Nexus. Operates Psycho-Temporal Clinic. Regular sessions with Team (individual and group). Advocate for neurodivergent rights across temporal civilization. Advisor to Vibrion on team psychology. Does not treat Dr. Entropy as patient — sees him as tragic, refuses to pathologize: "Brilliant man whose pain metastasized into ideology. He's wrong. That doesn't make him sick."

**Psychology & Personality:**

**Core Traits:**

1. **Warm:** Genuinely caring; patients feel seen
2. **Sharp:** Sees through deflection; gentle but persistent
3. **Advocate:** Fights for neurodivergent rights in policy and practice
4. **Calm:** Rarely flustered; grounding presence in crisis
5. **Curious:** Treats each person as unique; resists generalization
6. **Boundaried:** Professional; doesn't confuse care with over-involvement
7. **Hopeful:** Believes in growth; doesn't promise cure, promises possibility

**Motivations:**

- Help people thrive with their conditions (not despite them)
- Advance psycho-temporal field (research, teaching, advocacy)
- Support Team's emotional resilience (they carry heavy weight)
- Challenge stigma (neurodiversity as strength, not deficit)

**Fears & Vulnerabilities:**

- Team member in crisis she cannot reach (has happened; carries it)
- Her field being instrumentalized for control (Council has tried to use "assessment" for restriction)
- Burnout (cares deeply; must protect capacity)

**Coping Mechanisms:**

- Supervision and peer consultation
- Tea, garden walks, meditation
- Clear boundaries between work and personal
- Humor (light, never at patients' expense)

**Approach to Therapy:**

- **Neurodivergent-affirming:** Conditions are not problems to fix; they are differences to understand and leverage
- **Temporal-integrated:** Explores how time perception shapes experience, identity, ability
- **Strength-based:** Focus on what works; build from there
- **Collaborative:** Patient is expert on their experience; she brings framework
- **No cure narratives:** Thriving with condition, not recovery from it

**Relationships with Team Members:**

**With Vibrion:**
- Professional respect; he seeks her guidance on team dynamics
- He was initially skeptical of "psychological approach"; now appreciates it
- She helps him understand when to push, when to support, when to step back
- Leadership consultation, not therapy (he has own process)

**With Leo:**
- Understanding ADD-Immersive Thought connection
- Works with his tangential thinking (doesn't pathologize digressions)
- Strategies for focus when needed, acceptance when focus drifts
- He finds her non-judgmental; rare for him

**With Anne:**
- Anxiety-Foresight integration; understanding neuro-temporal nature
- Accepting she can't predict everything (control vs. adaptability)
- Strategies for when planning becomes paralysis
- She appreciates structure of sessions (predictability)

**With Maya:**
- Autism-Pattern Sense connection; identity and ability as integrated
- Social navigation strategies; requesting accommodations confidently
- Learning that literal communication is valid (not deficit)
- She values Dr. Mental's clarity and consistency

**With Eli:**
- ADHD-Kinetic Rush connection
- Energy management; when to channel, when to ground
- Not "managing" his energy down — directing it
- He appreciates she doesn't try to slow him into "normal"

**With Dave:**
- Depression-Empathic Resonance connection
- Managing depression without expecting cure
- Valuing his depth; low motivation isn't moral failing
- He finds her one of few who doesn't try to "fix" his heaviness

**With Iris:**
- Critical relationship: psychosis + Visionary Insight
- Distinguishing genuine temporal perception from hallucination
- Grounding strategies; when to trust perception, when to check
- Ongoing; Iris's condition requires sustained support

**With Dr. Volt:**
- Colleague; different domains
- She handles team psychology; he handles physics
- She says he "intellectualizes" — he admits it
- Mutual respect; occasional collaboration (temporal perception + mental health interface)

**With Dr. Entropy:**
- No therapeutic relationship
- She sees him as tragic: "Brilliant man whose pain metastasized into ideology"
- He dismisses her: "Soft science enabling soft minds"
- She would treat him if he asked; he would never ask

**With Antagonists (Enigma Volt, The Mentor):**
- Enigma: Would see him as trauma survivor; has not had opportunity
- Mentor: No documented contact
