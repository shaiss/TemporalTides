# Temporal Research Council

**Part V: Factions & Organizations**

---

## Quick Reference

**Official Name:** Temporal Research Council (TRC)
**Type:** Governing body for temporal research, stability, and enforcement
**Formation:** Early civilization (post-temporal fracture stabilization)
**Headquarters:** Nexus Tower, Chronopolis Central
**Status:** Dominant authority over baseline and ordered zones
**Leadership:** Council Chair (rotating); bureaucratic hierarchy

---

## 1. Organization Header

**Names:**
- Temporal Research Council (formal)
- "The Council" (colloquial)
- "The suits" (Enigma Volt)
- "Them" (autonomous zone suspicion)

**Formation:**
- Emerged from crisis (historical temporal fractures)
- Consolidated power through stabilization technology
- Chronopolis built as capital
- Temporal Accords established (framework for all zones)

**Current Status:**
- **Authority:** Over Chronopolis, baseline zones, research facilities
- **Size:** Thousands (bureaucracy, research, enforcement)
- **Territory:** Chronopolis (direct); influence across civilization
- **Crisis:** Dr. Entropy threat; forced cooperation with Team

---

## 2. Purpose & Goals

**Primary Mission:**
- Maintain temporal stability (prevent fractures, paradoxes)
- Enforce Temporal Accords (framework for zone relations)
- Conduct and regulate temporal research
- Protect citizens from temporal hazards

**Methods:**
- **Stabilization technology:** Arrays, anchors, monitoring
- **Regulation:** Research permits; ability registration; zone governance
- **Enforcement:** Temporal integrity division; gradient patrols
- **Surveillance:** Chronopolis model; "safety through monitoring"

**Philosophy:**
- Order prevents chaos
- Control enables safety
- Stability over freedom (trade-off explicit)
- "Better regulated than fractured"

---

## 3. History

**Founding:**
- Post-catastrophe (early temporal fractures)
- Scientists and administrators consolidated
- Stabilization tech developed (Dr. Volt's predecessors)
- Chronopolis established as secure capital

**Key Events:**
- Dr. Entropy (Marcus Theron) expelled (320 AC) — research confiscated
- Dr. Volt left Council employ (328 AC) — disillusionment
- Team formed (342 AC) — Council reluctantly accepts independent operatives
- Ongoing: Entropy threat forces unprecedented cooperation

---

## 4. Membership

**Structure:**
- Council seats (representatives from key zones)
- Bureaucracy (research, enforcement, administration)
- Temporal integrity division (field agents)
- Research institutes (Council-affiliated)

**Requirements:**
- Loyalty to Council
- Adherence to Accords
- Background checks
- Conformity (implicit)

---

## 5. Structure & Hierarchy

**Leadership:**
- Council Chair (elected from members)
- Division heads (research, enforcement, policy)
- Bureaucratic layers (standard government structure)

**Decision-Making:**
- Top-down
- Committees for technical matters
- Slow (deliberate; some say glacial)

---

## 6. Resources

**Funding:** Tax revenue; zone contributions; research grants
**Technology:** Best stabilization tech; monitoring systems; research facilities
**Territory:** Chronopolis (capital); influence over baseline zones
**Alliances:** Ordered zones; some structured zones; uneasy with autonomous

---

## 7. Philosophy & Culture

**Core Beliefs:**
- Order prevents catastrophe
- Regulation protects
- Control is compassion ("we prevent suffering")
- Stability requires sacrifice (of freedom)

**Reputation:**
- Chronopolis: Respected, feared
- Autonomous zones: Distrusted (control-seeking)
- Team: Useful but unreliable (won't follow orders)
- Dr. Entropy: Existential threat

---

## 8. Relationships

**Allies:** Ordered zones; Chronopolis residents; some research institutions
**Enemies:** Dr. Entropy; Entropy Forces
**Complex:** Team (cooperation necessary; trust limited); Autonomous zones (tension)
**Neutral:** Time Nexus (no authority there); Contemplative Sanctuaries (ignored)

---

## 9. Story Role

**Narrative Function:**
- ORDER extreme (thematic)
- Antagonist to autonomy (not villain, but opposition)
- Reluctant ally (Entropy threat forces partnership)
- Anne's origin (shaped her; she fled)

**Thematic:**
- Control vs. freedom
- Safety vs. spontaneity
- Not evil; wrong in degree
