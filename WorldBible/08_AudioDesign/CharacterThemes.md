# Character Themes

**Part VIII: Audio Design**

---

## Overview

Each character has a musical motif reflecting their personality, condition, and temporal signature. Themes should be recognizable in isolation and able to layer with others during team moments.

---

## Hero Themes

### Vibrion — "Resonance"
- **Genre:** Electronic rock + orchestral
- **Instrumentation:** Synthesizers, electric guitar, full orchestra
- **Tempo:** 130 BPM (driving, purposeful)
- **Key:** D major (power, brightness)
- **Reference:** Daft Punk meets Hans Zimmer
- **Character:** Strong beat = vibration; soaring melody = leadership
- **Variation:** Quiet version (piano + synth pad) for reflective moments

### Leo — "Tangent"
- **Genre:** Indie folk with time signature shifts
- **Instrumentation:** Acoustic guitar, piano, electronic flourishes
- **Tempo:** Variable (80-120 BPM; shifts mid-phrase)
- **Key:** G major → E minor (wandering, returning)
- **Reference:** Sufjan Stevens, Bon Iver
- **Character:** Meandering melody that suddenly crystallizes; unexpected pauses
- **Variation:** Deep focus version (stripped to piano, slowed)

### Anne — "Contingency"
- **Genre:** Minimalist orchestral with tension
- **Instrumentation:** Strings (precise), woodwinds, subtle electronic pulse
- **Tempo:** 100 BPM (controlled, measured; accelerates under stress)
- **Key:** A minor (anticipation, tension)
- **Reference:** Jonny Greenwood, Mica Levi
- **Character:** Orderly structure with mounting tension; resolves when plan succeeds
- **Variation:** Panic version (tempo doubles; strings frantic)

### Maya — "Sequence"
- **Genre:** Ambient electronic with mathematical structure
- **Instrumentation:** Synthesizers, tuned percussion, piano
- **Tempo:** Consistent 90 BPM (metronomic; never varies)
- **Key:** C major (clarity, structure)
- **Reference:** Nils Frahm, Olafur Arnalds
- **Character:** Repeating patterns that build; satisfaction when pattern completes
- **Variation:** Overload version (patterns layer too fast; dissonance)

### Eli — "Rush"
- **Genre:** Electronic/drum and bass with live drums
- **Instrumentation:** Drum kit, electronic beats, bass guitar, brass stabs
- **Tempo:** 160-180 BPM (fast; hyperfocus)
- **Key:** E major (energy, brightness)
- **Reference:** Pendulum, ODESZA, The Prodigy
- **Character:** Relentless energy; drops into focused groove during Kinetic Rush
- **Variation:** Resting version (just heartbeat rhythm; between bursts)

### Dave — "Resonance (Low)"
- **Genre:** Ambient/acoustic with musical depth
- **Instrumentation:** Guitar (acoustic), cello, piano, subtle reverb
- **Tempo:** 60 BPM (slow; weight in each note)
- **Key:** B-flat minor (melancholy, depth)
- **Reference:** Radiohead (piano), Max Richter, Sigur Ros
- **Character:** Heavy, beautiful; silence between notes matters; empathy = warmth entering
- **Variation:** Empathic version (adds warm strings when connecting with others)

### Iris — "Layers"
- **Genre:** Art music/experimental with shifting tonality
- **Instrumentation:** Glass harmonica, choir, reversed piano, field recordings
- **Tempo:** No fixed tempo (fluid, shifting)
- **Key:** Chromatic (no fixed key; moves between)
- **Reference:** Bjork, Hildur Gudnadottir, Meredith Monk
- **Character:** Multiple melodic lines simultaneously; some beautiful, some unsettling
- **Variation:** Grounded version (single melody emerges from layers)

## Mentor Themes

### Dr. Volt — "Inquiry"
- **Genre:** Contemporary classical (minimalist)
- **Instrumentation:** Piano (primary), quiet strings
- **Tempo:** 70 BPM (thoughtful)
- **Key:** F major (warmth within precision)
- **Character:** Quiet contemplation; gentle curiosity

### Dr. Mental — "Presence"
- **Genre:** Ambient/acoustic
- **Instrumentation:** Soft piano, acoustic guitar, nature sounds
- **Tempo:** 65 BPM (calming)
- **Key:** D-flat major (warmth, safety)
- **Character:** Safe space; invitation to rest

---

## Antagonist Themes

### Dr. Entropy — "Entropy"
- **Genre:** Dark orchestral with electronic distortion
- **Instrumentation:** Low brass, distorted strings, electronic decay
- **Tempo:** 90 BPM (methodical; inevitable)
- **Key:** D minor (gravity, conviction)
- **Character:** Not evil; tragic conviction; beauty in disorder

### Enigma Volt — "Static"
- **Genre:** Industrial/glitch
- **Instrumentation:** Electronic noise, distorted bass, disrupted signals
- **Tempo:** 140 BPM (agitation)
- **Key:** Atonal (disruption)
- **Character:** Pain and anger; interference patterns

---

## Team Theme — "Temporal Tides"
- **Combination:** All character motifs layered
- **Effect:** Different perceptions creating unified harmony
- **Usage:** Full team moments; victory; resonance points
