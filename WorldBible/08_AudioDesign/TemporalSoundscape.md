# Temporal Soundscape

**Part VIII: Audio Design**

---

## Overview

Time has sound. Different temporal zones, distortions, and abilities produce distinct audio signatures. This document defines how temporal phenomena translate to audio design.

---

## Zone Ambience by RFR

### Baseline (1.0 RFR)
- Natural environmental audio (wind, water, urban, rural)
- No temporal modification
- Reference standard

### Accelerated (>1.0 RFR)
- Pitch shifted slightly up (higher frequency)
- Compressed reverb (sounds tighter, closer)
- Increased density (more sound events per second)
- Subjective effect: urgency, alertness

### Dilated (<1.0 RFR)
- Pitch shifted slightly down (lower frequency)
- Extended reverb (sounds linger, echo)
- Decreased density (fewer events; silence valued)
- Subjective effect: contemplation, weight

### Chaotic (Variable RFR)
- Unpredictable pitch shifts
- Reverb stutters and breaks
- Sounds from multiple timelines overlapping
- Causality errors (echo before source)
- Subjective effect: disorientation, wrongness

---

## Temporal Effect Audio

### Gradient Crossing
- Pitch slide (smooth transition)
- "Temporal hum" increases then resolves
- Duration reflects gradient steepness

### Temporal Echo
- Delayed repeat of past sounds
- Slight pitch offset (not exact copy)
- Ghostly; present but distant

### Wormhole
- Low bass rumble (sub-frequency)
- Air displacement sound (whoosh)
- Reality-tear crackle (entrance/exit)

### Paradox
- Dissonant chord (wrong notes; wrong timing)
- Audio stutter (time catching itself)
- Brief silence (causality gap)

### Timeline Fracture
- Glass-breaking sound (reality shattering)
- Multiple versions of same sound overlapping
- Nausea-inducing phase cancellation

## Location Audio Signatures

### Time Nexus
- Crystal resonance (harmonic drone)
- Multiple zone echoes (layered ambience)
- Warm; welcoming; ancient

### Chronopolis Central
- Urban hum (machinery, transport)
- Surveillance tones (subtle; present)
- Orderly; sterile; pressured

### Contemplative Sanctuaries
- Natural quiet (wind, water, birdsong — slowed)
- Extended reverb (sounds linger)
- Musical; contemplative

### Accelerated Quarter
- Dense urban noise (compressed)
- Electronic beats (ambient)
- Urgent; exciting; overwhelming

### Fractured Wastes
- Silence that screams
- Reversed audio
- Impossible sounds (wrong direction, wrong time)
- Horror register

### Dr. Entropy's Lair
- Controlled static
- Catalyst hum (ominous, low)
- Paradox crackles
- Philosophical: chaos with pattern
