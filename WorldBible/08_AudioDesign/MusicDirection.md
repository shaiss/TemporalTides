# Music Direction

**Part VIII: Audio Design**

---

## Overview

Temporal Tides' music bridges genres — electronic, orchestral, ambient, experimental — reflecting the universe's diversity of perception. Music is adaptive: it responds to zone, character, emotional state, and narrative moment.

---

## Overall Musical Identity

### Core Sound
- **Foundation:** Orchestral (emotion, scale)
- **Layer:** Electronic (temporal, modern)
- **Texture:** Ambient (atmosphere, space)
- **Accent:** Genre-specific per character (identity)

### Genre Blend
- Not a single genre; a blend reflecting neurodiversity
- Different characters bring different musical traditions
- Team moments layer genres into harmony
- Reference palette: Hans Zimmer + Radiohead + Nils Frahm + ODESZA

---

## Adaptive Music System

### Zone Adaptation
Music modulates based on current temporal zone:
- **Baseline:** Standard instrumentation and tempo
- **Accelerated:** Tempo increases; density increases; brighter
- **Dilated:** Tempo decreases; reverb extends; deeper
- **Chaotic:** Unpredictable modulation; layers conflict

### Character Adaptation
When controlling/following a character, their motif leads:
- Other motifs present (at lower volume)
- Transitions smooth (crossfade over 2-4 seconds)
- Emotional state affects intensity

### Narrative Adaptation
- **Exploration:** Ambient; atmospheric; zone-appropriate
- **Puzzle-solving:** Character motif + focused intensity
- **Combat/action:** Full orchestral + electronic; high energy
- **Emotional/dialogue:** Stripped; subtle; character-specific
- **Climactic:** All motifs layered; team theme

---

## Emotional Palette

### Joy/Victory
- Major keys; full instrumentation
- Team theme fragments
- Rising resolution

### Tension/Danger
- Minor keys; dissonance
- Reduced instrumentation (solo instruments)
- Tempo shifts

### Sadness/Loss
- Dave's register (low, reverberant)
- Solo piano or cello
- Extended silence between phrases

### Wonder/Discovery
- Iris's register (layered, chromatic)
- New instruments introduced
- Open harmonic space

### Determination
- Vibrion's beat (driving, purposeful)
- Full ensemble building
- Forward momentum

---

## Technical Guidelines

### Looping
- Music loops seamlessly for exploration
- Transitions between loop states via stems
- No jarring cuts

### Dynamic Mixing
- 4-8 stems per zone (layered/unlayered by context)
- Character motif always present (low level)
- Environmental stems respond to RFR

### Silence
- Silence is a musical tool
- Dave's moments; Contemplative Sanctuaries
- Fractured Wastes: silence that feels wrong
