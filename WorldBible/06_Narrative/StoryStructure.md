# Story Structure

**Part VI: Narrative Design**

---

## Overview

Temporal Tides uses the **Non-Linear Resonance Model** — a four-act structure that mirrors the universe's temporal mechanics: characters begin in divergence (separate experiences), converge through conflict, achieve resonance (harmony through diversity), and reflect on transformation. Unlike linear three-act structure, resonance points can occur throughout; character arcs interweave across perceived time.

---

## The Four Acts

### Act I — Divergence (Chapters 1-5)

**Function:** Establish each character's separate temporal experience and unique perception of time.

**Focus:**
- Individual perspectives; each character's world
- Isolation (before Team forms)
- Discovery (of abilities, of threat, of each other)
- "How do I experience time differently?"

**Narrative Style:**
- Multiple parallel or sequential POVs
- Each chapter anchored in one character's perception
- Time may feel different per chapter (Leo's languid vs. Eli's rapid)
- Stakes: Dr. Entropy's threat emerging; Team assembly begins

**Key Beats:**
- Vibrion recognizes need for team
- First recruits (Leo, Anne)
- Dr. Entropy's operations escalate
- Each character's temporal signature established

**Emotional Arc:** Isolation → Discovery

---

### Act II — Convergence (Chapters 6-12)

**Function:** Bring characters together; reveal common purpose; learn to work across different perceptions.

**Focus:**
- Team dynamics; learning each other's rhythms
- Conflict (miscommunication from different time perceptions)
- Understanding (Dr. Mental's role; growth)
- "How can we synchronize despite our differences?"

**Narrative Style:**
- Interweaving storylines; cause and effect across timestreams
- Team missions (coordinated action)
- The Mentor's complexity revealed (connection to Entropy)
- Crisis: Attack on Nexus (Chapter 11); Interchange disruption (Chapter 12)

**Key Beats:**
- Full Team assembled (Dave, Iris joined)
- First major failure (learn from it)
- Dr. Entropy's strategy understood
- Mentor betrayal/complexity (Chapter 6)
- Nexus attack (Chapter 11)

**Emotional Arc:** Conflict → Understanding

---

### Act III — Resonance (Chapters 13-18)

**Function:** Achieve harmony through diversity; leverage abilities in concert; confront Dr. Entropy.

**Focus:**
- Team synergy (abilities combined)
- Infiltration (Lair approach)
- Final confrontation (Chronos Catalyst)
- "What becomes possible when we work together?"

**Narrative Style:**
- Unified timeline with multiple temporal layers
- High-stakes action; puzzle-solving; character moments
- Dr. Entropy's philosophy confronted (not merely fought)
- Sacrifice and triumph

**Key Beats:**
- Preparation for Lair (Chapters 13-14)
- Fractured Wastes mission (materials; intel) (Chapter 15)
- Infiltration (Chapters 16-17)
- Catalyst confrontation (Chapter 18)

**Emotional Arc:** Unity → Triumph

---

### Act IV — Reflection (Chapters 19-20)

**Function:** Process the journey; character growth; lasting change.

**Focus:**
- Personal growth; what changed
- Aftermath; new equilibrium
- Hope; future possibilities
- "How have we changed, and what does that mean for the future?"

**Narrative Style:**
- Character-focused vignettes
- Multiple POVs (each character's reflection)
- Open endings (potential for sequel)
- Thematic closure

**Key Beats:**
- Catalyst disabled; Entropy contained
- Team processes (Dr. Mental; support)
- Individual character resolutions
- Hope for balance (order and chaos)

**Emotional Arc:** Resolution → Hope

---

## Resonance Points

**Definition:** Moments when character arcs, plot, and theme align — regardless of act. Non-linear structure allows resonance at any point.

**Examples:**
- Leo's Immersive Thought solving impossible puzzle (resonance: different thinking = strength)
- Dave's empathy reaching Enigma (resonance: connection across opposition)
- Anne's Foresight + team execution (resonance: planning + trust)
- Vibrion delegating to team (resonance: leadership = empowerment)
