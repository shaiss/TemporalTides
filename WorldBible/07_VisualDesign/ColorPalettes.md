# Color Palettes

**Part VII: Visual Design**

---

## Overview

Color is identity in Temporal Tides. Each character and temporal zone has a distinct palette that communicates personality, condition, and temporal properties at a glance. These palettes should be consistent across all media.

---

## Zone Palettes

### Time Nexus
- Crystalline whites and silvers
- Refracted rainbow (past/present/future)
- Deep blue (anchor glow)
- Warmth in inhabited areas

### Chronopolis Central
- Blue-white (#E8F0FE) — order, cleanliness
- Steel gray (#708090)
- Glass reflections
- Cold; professional

### Baseline Metropolitan
- Natural variety (no imposed scheme)
- Neighborhood variation
- Earth tones, warm colors
- Lived-in; organic

### Accelerated Quarter
- Neon (blue #00BFFF, pink #FF69B4, green #39FF14, orange #FF6600)
- Black backgrounds
- High contrast; flashing
- Urgency; energy

### Contemplative Sanctuaries
- Earth tones (brown #8B7355, green #556B2F, gray #A9A9A9)
- Natural materials
- Muted; soft; warm
- Seasonal shifts

### Fractured Wastes
- Wrong colors (temporal spectrum shifted)
- No consistent palette (chaos)
- Impossible hues
- Unnatural; unsettling

### Dr. Entropy's Lair
- Dark (near-black)
- Crimson (Catalyst glow)
- Flickering instability
- Multiple overlapping palettes

### The Interchange
- Gradient spectrum (slow warm → baseline neutral → fast cool)
- Market colors (vibrant; commercial)
- Three-district variation
